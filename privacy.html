Privacy Policy

Redia: Rate Media · Effective and last updated September 28, 2026

The short version
  • Redia signs you in with your iCloud account. We never see your Apple ID email address, name or password.
  • Your username, display name, optional profile photo, ratings, reviews and friends list are stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) and are visible to other Redia users. That's how friends can find you and see what you've rated.
  • To show you titles and artwork, searches and lookups are sent to the catalogs Redia uses (TMDB, Apple Music, Open Library, AniList and IGDB), without any information about who you are.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and your data is never sold.
  • You can delete your account, and everything you've posted, in the app at any time.

Who we are

Redia is made by Omar Saraireh, an independent developer based in Canada ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, email omorasaraira@gmail.com.

What Redia stores and why

Your iCloud identity

Redia uses Apple's CloudKit, so you sign in with the iCloud account already on your iPhone. Apple gives Redia an anonymous identifier that is unique to Redia. It does not give us your Apple ID email address, your real name or your password. We use this identifier only to know which profile, ratings and friends are yours.

Your public profile

  • Username (required, unique), so friends can find you.
  • Display name (required). You choose it; it doesn't have to be your real name.
  • Profile photo (optional). If you add one, you pick it from your photos; Redia receives only the photo you choose, not your photo library.

Your ratings and reviews

You can rate movies, TV shows, albums, songs, books, comics, manga and video games, as well as actors, directors and authors. Each rating stores:

  • the item's catalog ID (for example "movie:550" from TMDB or "book:OL45883W" from Open Library) and its type;
  • for albums and songs, the item's industry code (UPC or ISRC), so your rating still matches if Apple Music uses a different ID in another country;
  • your score, your optional review, and the date.

We store only these IDs, never copies of the catalogs' titles, artwork, descriptions or scores. Ratings of people are public in the same way as ratings of titles.

Friends

When you add a friend, Redia saves a record that you follow that user. Adding is one-way, like a follow: the other person doesn't need to approve it, and they can see that you added them.

Blocks and reports

If you block someone, Redia saves that so their profile and content are hidden from you. If you report a user or a review, Redia saves the report (who reported whom, which content, and the reason you chose) so that we can review it. Reports are used only for moderation.

Your library is semi-public: it isn't listed or indexed on the web, but any Redia user who knows or finds your username can view it. Please don't put anything in your display name or reviews that you wouldn't want other people to read.

Where it's stored

All of the above is stored in Redia's public database in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Apple hosts it and may store it on servers outside your country, including in the United States. It doesn't use your personal iCloud storage. We can see these records through Apple's developer tools, and we look at them only to run the app, handle reports, and answer your requests.

Your category settings

The categories you switch on or off, and the order you put them in on Home, are saved only on your iPhone. We never receive them.

Titles, artwork and people (the catalogs)

Redia doesn't have its own database of titles. It asks these catalogs directly from your iPhone:

  • TMDB (The Movie Database): movies, TV shows, actors and directors. To show what's in theatres near you, Redia also sends TMDB your iPhone's region setting (for example "CA").
  • Apple Music (through Apple's MusicKit): albums and songs.
  • Open Library (openlibrary.org, a project of the Internet Archive): books, comics and authors.
  • AniList (anilist.co): manga, manhwa and manhua.
  • IGDB (igdb.com, owned by Twitch): video games.

When you search, browse Home or open a page, your iPhone sends the relevant catalog what it needs to answer: the text you searched for, or the ID of the item or person, and it downloads artwork from the catalog's image servers. Redia only asks the catalogs for the categories you have switched on. These requests contain no username, iCloud identifier or other information about who you are. Like any website or service you connect to, each catalog can see your device's IP address.

To keep the app fast and working offline, catalog responses and artwork are cached on your iPhone for no more than 30 days, then refreshed or deleted.

App configuration

When Redia starts, it downloads small configuration records from our iCloud database (the keys the app uses to access TMDB and IGDB) and keeps them in your iPhone's Keychain. They contain nothing about you.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No in-app purchases or subscriptions.
  • No access to your location, contacts, microphone, camera or music library.
  • We never sell, rent or trade your data.

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Your account and content stay until you delete them. You can edit or delete any rating or review at any time.
  • Delete your account in the app: go to Profile, then Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, photo, username, ratings (including ratings of people), reviews, friends list and blocks from Redia's database, and frees up your username. Other users' friend lists will no longer show you.
  • Reports you made, or that were made about you, may be kept for up to 12 months after they're resolved, in case the same problem comes up again, then deleted.
  • Deleting the app removes the on-device cache, but it does not delete your account. Use Delete Account first, or email us.
  • By email: if you can't use the app, email omorasaraira@gmail.com with your username and we'll delete your account within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request from inside the app.
  • With the catalogs (TMDB, Apple, Open Library, AniList, IGDB): see their privacy policies, linked above, for how long they keep request logs.

Permissions

Redia needs to be signed in to iCloud to create a profile. You can control that in Settings > [your name] > iCloud, and whether Redia may use mobile data in Settings > Redia.

Apple Music (Media & Apple Music): Redia may ask for this the first time you open music, because Apple requires it to search the Apple Music catalog. It never asks at launch. Redia uses it only to look up albums and songs; it doesn't read your music library or playlists, and you don't need an Apple Music subscription to browse and rate. If you say no, everything except music still works. You can change it any time in Settings > Redia.

Safety and user content

Usernames, display names, photos and reviews are written by users. You can block any user to hide their content, and report users or reviews that break the rules (for example harassment, hate speech or explicit content). We review reports and may remove content or accounts that break these rules.

Children's privacy

Redia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, if higher). Because profiles and ratings are visible to other users, we recommend that younger teens use Redia only with a parent's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the EU and UK GDPR and California's CCPA give you rights over your personal information, including to access it, correct it, delete it, get a copy of it, or object to how it is used. You can see and edit almost everything directly in the app, and delete it all with Delete Account. For anything else, email omorasaraira@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

We process your information to provide the app you asked for (your profile, ratings and friends) and, for reports, because of our legitimate interest in keeping Redia safe. You may also complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, trackers or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If Redia's data practices change, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. For significant changes, we'll also mention it in the app's release notes on the App Store.
