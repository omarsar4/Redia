Privacy Policy

Redia: Rate Media · Effective and last updated October 28, 2026

The short version
  • Redia signs you in with your iCloud account. We never see your Apple ID email address, name or password.
  • Your username, display name, bio, profile photo, ratings, reviews, and who you follow are stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) and are visible to other Redia users. That's how friends can find you and see what you've rated.
  • Who you block, and any reports you send, are private.
  • To show you titles and artwork, searches and lookups are sent to the catalogs Redia uses (TMDB, Apple Music, Open Library, AniList and IGDB), without any information about who you are.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and your data is never sold.
  • You can delete your account, and everything you've posted, in the app at any time.

Who we are

Redia is made by Omar Saraireh, an independent developer based in Canada ("we", "us"). Redia is free and non-commercial. If you have any question about this policy or your data, email omorasaraira@gmail.com.

What Redia stores and why

Redia has no servers of its own. Your account data is stored in Redia's database in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit).

Your iCloud identity

You sign in with the iCloud account already on your iPhone. Apple gives Redia an anonymous identifier that is unique to Redia. It does not give us your Apple ID email address, your real name or your password. We use this identifier only to know which profile, ratings and follows are yours.

Your profile

  • Username (required and unique), so friends can find you. You can change it once every 30 days. When you do, your old username is held for 14 days so nobody can use it to pretend to be you, then it's released. We store the date of your last change to enforce this.
  • Display name (optional). You choose it; it doesn't have to be your real name. If you leave it empty, your username is shown.
  • Bio (optional), up to 150 characters, shown on your profile.
  • Profile photo (optional). You pick one photo with Apple's photo picker, so Redia only ever receives that photo, never the rest of your library. Before uploading, Redia crops it to a square, resizes it, and re-saves it, which removes hidden information such as the location where it was taken. It's uploaded only when you tap Save.

Usernames, display names, bios and reviews containing hateful language are rejected.

Your ratings and reviews

You can rate movies, TV shows, albums, songs, books, comics, manga and video games, as well as actors, directors, authors and music artists. Each rating stores:

  • the item's catalog ID (for example "movie:550" from TMDB or "book:OL45883W" from Open Library) and its type;
  • for albums and songs, the item's industry code (UPC or ISRC), so your rating still matches if Apple Music uses a different ID in another country;
  • your score, your optional review, and the date.

We store only these IDs, never copies of the catalogs' titles, artwork, descriptions or scores.

Following

When you follow someone, Redia saves a record that you follow them. Following is one-way: the other person doesn't need to approve it. Your followers and the people you follow appear on your profile.

Blocks, reports and review flags

  • Blocks. If you block someone, Redia saves that so their profile, reviews and ratings are hidden from you, and unfollows them. Your blocks are private: other users can't see them.
  • Reports. If you report a user, a review or a profile photo, Redia saves the report: who sent it, who it's about, the item (for a review), the reason you chose, any details you add, and the date. Reports are private: only you and our moderators can see them, and they're used only for moderation.
  • Review flags. Reporting a review also adds a flag to that review. A review flagged by three different people is hidden for everyone until a moderator looks at it. The app doesn't show who flagged a review, but the flag records themselves are stored in Redia's public database alongside reviews.

Where it's stored

All of the above is stored in Redia's database in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Apple hosts it and may store it on servers outside your country, including in the United States. It doesn't use your personal iCloud storage. We can see these records through Apple's developer tools, and we look at them only to run the app, handle reports, and answer your requests.

On your device only

  • Your settings: which categories are on, their order on Home, and whether strong language is hidden.
  • Cached catalog data: posters, covers and details are kept on your iPhone for at most 30 days so the app is fast and works offline. You can clear them any time in Settings > Storage. Profile photos of other users are cached for a day.
  • App configuration: the keys the app uses to reach TMDB and IGDB, downloaded from our iCloud database and kept in your iPhone's Keychain. They contain nothing about you.

We never receive any of this.

Catalog services

Redia doesn't have its own database of titles. To show titles, artwork and public ratings, it contacts these services directly from your iPhone:

ServiceUsed forIts privacy information
TMDB (The Movie Database) Movies, TV shows, actors and directors TMDB Privacy Policy
Apple Music (Apple's MusicKit) Albums, songs and artists Apple Privacy Policy
Open Library (Internet Archive) Books, comics and authors Internet Archive Terms and Privacy
AniList Manga, manhwa and manhua AniList Terms and Privacy
IGDB (owned by Twitch) Video games Twitch Privacy Notice

When you search, browse Home or open a page, these services receive your search terms, the items you open, and, like any internet request, your device's IP address. To show what's in theatres near you, TMDB also receives your iPhone's region setting (for example "CA"). Redia only contacts the catalogs for the categories you have switched on. No account information is sent to them: never your username, iCloud identifier, ratings, reviews or follows.

Apple Music permission

The first time you open music in Redia and tap Allow Apple Music, iOS asks whether Redia may use Apple Music. Apple requires this before any app can look up its music catalog, and Redia never asks for it at launch. Redia uses it only to search and show catalog information. It never reads your music library or listening history, never plays music, and never changes anything in your Apple Music account. You don't need an Apple Music subscription. If you decline, everything except music keeps working, and you can change your mind in Settings > Redia. "Open in Apple Music" simply opens the item in the Music app.

Moderation

To keep Redia welcoming:

  • Reviews, usernames, display names and bios containing slurs can't be saved.
  • Swearing in other people's reviews and bios is masked (for example "f***") unless you turn off Settings > Safety > Hide Strong Language. Your own reviews are never changed.
  • You can report a user, a review, a profile photo or a bio, and block anyone.
  • We aim to review reports within 24 hours. Abusive reviews, profile photos, bios or accounts may be removed.

Our moderators receive a notification when a new report arrives. Redia never asks anyone else for notification permission, and doesn't send you notifications.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No in-app purchases or subscriptions.
  • No access to your location, contacts, microphone, camera, photo library or music library.
  • No collection of your email, phone number or payment information.
  • We never sell, rent or trade your data.

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Your account and content stay until you delete them. You can change or remove any rating or review at any time, and change or remove your display name, bio and photo.
  • Delete your account in the app: go to Settings > Delete Account and type your username to confirm. This permanently deletes your profile, photo and bio, your ratings and reviews, your follows, your blocks, the reports and flags you've sent, and frees your username, including any old username still being held. Other people's follower and following lists stop showing you.
  • Deleting the app removes the on-device cache and settings, but it does not delete your account. Use Delete Account first, or email us.
  • By email: if you can't use the app, email omorasaraira@gmail.com with your username and we'll delete your account within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request from inside the app.
  • Reports about you sent by other people are kept only as long as needed to deal with them.
  • With the catalogs: see their privacy policies, linked above, for how long they keep request logs.

Permissions

  • iCloud: Redia needs you to be signed in to iCloud to create a profile. Settings > [your name] > iCloud.
  • Apple Music (only if you open music): Settings > Redia > Media & Apple Music.
  • Photos: none needed. Apple's photo picker lets you choose a single photo without giving Redia access to your library.

Children's privacy

Redia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, if higher). Because profiles and ratings are visible to other users, we recommend that younger teens use Redia only with a parent's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the EU and UK GDPR and California's CCPA give you rights over your personal information, including to access it, correct it, delete it, get a copy of it, or object to how it is used. You can see and edit almost everything directly in the app, and delete it all with Delete Account. For anything else, email omorasaraira@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

We process your information to provide the app you asked for (your profile, ratings and follows) and, for moderation, because of our legitimate interest in keeping Redia safe. You may also complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
