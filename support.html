Frequently asked questions

Getting started

Do I need to create an account?

No password needed. Redia uses the iCloud account on your iPhone. The first time you open it, you choose the categories you're into, then pick a username and a display name, and that's it.

It says I'm not signed in to iCloud.
  • Open Settings and make sure you're signed in with your Apple Account at the top.
  • In Settings > [your name] > iCloud, make sure iCloud Drive is turned on.
  • Check that you're connected to the internet, then reopen Redia.
Can I change my username or display name?

Yes. Go to Profile and tap Edit. Usernames must be unique, so if the one you want is taken you'll need to pick another.

How do I turn categories on or off, or change their order?

Go to Profile > Categories & Home Order. Switch off anything you're not into, and drag the rest into the order you want. Home, search and your Library follow that order. At least one category stays on.

Switching a category off only hides it. Your ratings in it are kept and come back when you switch it on again.

Ratings

What can I rate?

Movies, TV shows, albums, songs, books, comics, manga (including manhwa and manhua) and video games. You can also rate actors, directors and authors from their pages.

How do ratings work?

Ratings go from half a star to five stars, in half-star steps, the same for everything. A review is optional. You can change or delete a rating at any time from the item's page or your Library.

What's the gold score next to a title?

That's the community score from the catalog the title comes from, shown on that catalog's own scale: TMDB out of 10, Open Library out of 5, AniList as a percentage, and IGDB out of 100. Your own rating is always shown in red stars, so the two never get mixed up. Apple Music doesn't publish ratings, so music shows charts instead.

I can't find something.

Try the original title or a shorter search, and check that its category is switched on. Each category comes from a different catalog:

  • Movies, TV shows, actors and directors: TMDB
  • Music: the Apple Music catalog
  • Books, Western comics and authors: Open Library
  • Manga, manhwa and manhua: AniList
  • Video games: IGDB

If something isn't in its catalog, Redia can't show it yet. TMDB, Open Library, AniList and IGDB are all community-edited, so anyone can add missing items there. Adult-only titles are left out on purpose.

An item's details or artwork are wrong.

Details and artwork come from the catalogs above, and Redia refreshes them from time to time. If something is wrong in the catalog itself, it can be corrected there.

Do I need Apple Music to rate music?

No. You can browse and rate albums and songs without an Apple Music subscription. The first time you open music, Redia may ask for access to Apple Music. That's only to search Apple's catalog; Redia doesn't read your library or playlists.

Friends and privacy

How do I add a friend?

Open the Friends tab, search for their username and tap Add. Adding is one-way, like a follow, so they don't have to accept. Their ratings then show up in your Friends tab.

Who can see my ratings?

Your profile, ratings and reviews are visible to other Redia users who look up your username. They aren't published on the web. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I block or report someone?

Open the person's profile, tap the ••• button, then choose Block or Report. You can also report an individual review. Blocked users are hidden from you. Reports are reviewed by the developer, and content or accounts that break the rules are removed.

To unblock someone, go to Profile > Blocked Users.

Someone is harassing me or posting something harmful.

Block and report them in the app. If it's urgent, also email omorasaraira@gmail.com with their username. If anyone is in danger, contact your local emergency services.

Your account

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile > Delete Account and confirm. This permanently deletes your profile, photo, ratings (including ratings of people), reviews and friends list, and frees up your username. It can't be undone.

Just deleting the app doesn't delete your account. If you've already deleted it, email omorasaraira@gmail.com with your username and I'll delete your account within 30 days.

I got a new iPhone. Will my ratings come with me?

Yes. Sign in with the same iCloud account and everything will be there.

Does Redia cost anything?

No. Redia is free, with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
