How it works

  1. Pick a usernameRedia uses your iCloud account, so there's no password to create. Choose a username and a display name.

  2. Rate what you watchSearch for a title, give it stars and, if you like, a short review. It lands in your Library.

  3. Add friendsSearch for a friend's username and add them. Adding is one-way, like a follow, so there are no requests to wait on.

  4. See what they thinkOpen the Friends tab to browse your friends' ratings and reviews.

Good to know: your profile and ratings are visible to other Redia users, so friends can find and follow you. Keep that in mind when choosing a display name or writing reviews.
