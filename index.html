Everything you love, in one place

Rate anything, precisely

Movies, TV shows, albums, songs, books, comics, manga and video games. Slide across the stars to rate in quarter-star steps, with a gentle tick at every step, and add a review if you like.

Rate the people too

Actors, directors, authors and musicians get their own pages, with their photo, biography and body of work, sorted by role, from acting to directing to writing. Rate them just like a title.

Always something new

Swipe through what's trending and top rated in every category: what's in theatres, on TV this week, the top songs and albums, and the most anticipated games.

Search everything at once

One search covers titles, actors, directors, authors and artists. Narrow it down with a tap: Movies, TV, Music, Books, Comics & Manga, Games or People.

The full story

Cast and crew for films and shows, tracklists for albums, authors for books, creators for manga, developers and platforms for games, plus each catalog's community score, kept separate from your own stars.

Your profile

Everything you've rated, all together in one beautiful grid, or sorted into collections: movies, shows, actors, directors, albums, songs, artists, books, authors, comics, manga and games. Add a photo and a short bio, and see your followers and your average up top.

Follow your friends

Find friends by username and follow them. Their ratings and reviews show up on the pages for titles, people and authors, so you know what they loved before you press play.

Make it yours

Only into films and manga? Switch off the categories you don't care about and drag the rest into the order you want on Home. Your ratings are always kept.

Friendly by design

Hateful language can't be posted, and swearing in others' reviews is hidden unless you turn it on. Block or report anyone. No ads, no tracking, and you can delete your account at any time. Read the privacy policy.

iPhone Duo

Made for every screen, including iPhone Duo.

Open iPhone Duo and Redia opens with it. On the big inner display, your profile grid spreads out into more columns, and title, people and search pages sit comfortably in the centre instead of stretching edge to edge.

Redia runs full screen on both of iPhone Duo's displays, and works in portrait and landscape on every iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

How it works

  1. Choose what you're intoPick the categories you want, from films to manga to games. You can change them any time in Settings.

  2. Pick a usernameRedia uses your iCloud account, so there's no password to create. Choose a username, and add a display name, photo and short bio if you like.

  3. Rate as you goSearch for anything, slide to rate it and, if you like, add a few words. It all lands on your profile.

  4. Follow your friendsFind friends by username and follow them, with no requests to wait on. Their ratings appear right alongside yours.

Good to know: your profile, ratings and reviews are visible to other Redia users, so friends can find and follow you. Keep that in mind when choosing a display name, photo or bio, or writing reviews.
