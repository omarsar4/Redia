Everything you love, in one place

Rate anything

Movies, TV shows, albums, songs, books, comics, manga and video games. Five stars in half-star steps, the same for everything, plus an optional review.

Rate the people too

Actors, directors and authors get their own pages, with a photo, biography and full body of work, and you can rate them just like a title.

Always something new

Swipe through what's trending and top rated in every category, plus what's in theatres, on TV this week, and the most anticipated upcoming games.

Search everything at once

One search covers titles, actors, directors and authors. Narrow it down with a tap: Movies, TV, Music, Books, Comics & Manga, Games or People.

The full story

Cast and crew for every film and show, authors and subjects for books, creators for manga, developers and platforms for games, and community scores from each catalog.

Your library

Every rating in one place, filtered by category with counts, sorted by most recent or highest rated, with your stats at a glance.

Make it yours

Only into films and manga? Switch off the categories you don't care about and drag the rest into the order you want on Home. Your ratings are always kept.

Friends

Add friends by username and browse their rated libraries. See what they loved before you press play, open the book or pick up the controller.

No ads. No tracking.

Sign in with your iCloud account, no passwords. Block or report anyone, and delete your account in the app at any time. Read the privacy policy.

How it works

  1. Choose what you're intoPick the categories you want, from films to manga to games. You can change them any time.

  2. Pick a usernameRedia uses your iCloud account, so there's no password to create. Choose a username and a display name.

  3. Rate as you goSearch for anything, give it stars and, if you like, a few words. It all lands in your Library.

  4. Follow your friendsAdd friends by username, one-way like a follow, and browse everything they've rated in the Friends tab.

Good to know: your profile and ratings are visible to other Redia users, so friends can find and follow you. Keep that in mind when choosing a display name or writing reviews.
